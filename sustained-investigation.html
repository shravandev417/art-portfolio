Sustained Investigation

The AP curriculum asks artists to explore a central idea through a sustained investigation. Mine is inspired by the political works of European artists featured in the Rijksmuseum, contemporary political art, and comic and animated work from around the world.

I am investigating how human-animal hybrid and augmented anatomy can express the emotions and effects that war and stress bring out in people. My investigation question will be finalized later in the year.
